PRODUCTION PLANTS

1. Manufacturing & Industrial

Heavy plant machinery, industrial replacement parts, raw materials, and factory output requiring dependable scheduled truckloads.

Typical Cargo: Machine tools, gearboxes, pumps, casting molds, fabricated assemblies.
Transportation Needs: High floor weight rating, heavy straps, crane or forklift access.
Relevant Equipment: Heavy Dry Van or Flatbed Trailer.
Enquire for Manufacturing
JOB SITE LOGISTICS

2. Construction & Infrastructure

Oversized, rugged materials moved directly to infrastructure project sites, highway bridge builds, and commercial developments.

Typical Cargo: Steel rebar, scaffolding, precast concrete beams, drainage pipes.
Transportation Needs: Open-deck access, heavy chain binders, off-highway site delivery.
Relevant Equipment: Flatbed Trailers & Drop-Decks.
Enquire for Construction
AGRI-COMMODITIES

3. Agriculture & Agricultural Products

Bulk agricultural produce, bagged grains, organic fertilizers, and farm equipment requiring agile seasonal dispatch across harvest belts.

Typical Cargo: Bagged wheat, corn, pulses, agricultural seeds, organic nutrients.
Transportation Needs: Moisture isolation, tarp cover, high cubic capacity.
Relevant Equipment: Enclosed Dry Vans & Tarped Flatbeds.
Enquire for Agriculture
TIER-1 OEM LINES

4. Automotive & Auto Components

High-precision just-in-time delivery for Tier-1 and Tier-2 automotive component manufacturers supporting vehicle assembly plants.

Typical Cargo: Automotive stamping panels, engine blocks, wiring harnesses, tires.
Transportation Needs: Strict time-window docking, air-ride suspension, returnable container racks.
Relevant Equipment: Dedicated 53ft Air-Ride Dry Vans.
Enquire for Automotive
FULFILLMENT HUBS

5. Retail & Consumer Goods

High-turnover consumer merchandise, electronics, textiles, and household goods moving between regional distribution centers and stores.

Typical Cargo: Boxed apparel, footwear, home appliances, packaged consumables.
Transportation Needs: High cubic volume, tamper-evident security seals, dock levelers.
Relevant Equipment: 53ft High-Cube Dry Vans.
Enquire for Retail
HEAVY METALS

6. Steel & Metal Products

High-density steel coils, metal ingots, aluminum extrusions, and heavy plate steel requiring specialized coil bunks and heavy chains.

Typical Cargo: Master steel coils (eye-to-sky / eye-to-wall), I-beams, metal sheets.
Transportation Needs: Coil racks, beveled dunnage, high-grade Grade-70 chain binders.
Relevant Equipment: Heavy-Duty Flatbed Trailers.
Enquire for Steel
FAST-MOVING CONSUMABLES

7. FMCG & Distribution

Fast-moving consumer goods, packaged personal care, beverages, and ambient shelf products distributed on rigid turnaround cycles.

Typical Cargo: Bottled beverages, hygiene products, packaged dry groceries.
Transportation Needs: Standard pallet footprint, shrink-wrap protection, multi-stop drops.
Relevant Equipment: Dry Vans & Medium Distribution Trucks.
Enquire for FMCG
PACKAGING LOGISTICS

8. Packaging & Paper Products

Corrugated cartons, industrial paper master rolls, folding cartons, and flexible plastic films demanding moisture-free transport.

Typical Cargo: Paper mill reels, flattened corrugated sheets, bulk protective packaging.
Transportation Needs: Clean dry interiors, roll clamps or roll chocks, friction mats.
Relevant Equipment: Enclosed Dry Freight Vans.
Enquire for Packaging
