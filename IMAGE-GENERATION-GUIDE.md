## Detailed Image Generation Specifications

```
Master Aesthetic Directive:
All images must reflect ultra-realistic commercial vehicle photography, shot on medium-format cameras (Hasselblad H6D-100c / Phase One XF, 50mm or 85mm prime lenses, f/5.6 - f/8 for crisp edge-to-edge depth). Clean industrial colour grade with deep navy shadows (#101318, #142337), cool metallic steel blues, and selective signal orange accents.
CRITICAL VEHICLE RULES: Strict adherence to real-world commercial vehicle proportions (correct tandem axles, fuel tanks, fifth wheels, cabover / conventional cab geometry, air-suspension clearance). Absolutely NO visible brand emblems (Volvo, Scania, Peterbilt, Kenworth, Tata, BharatBenz, Freightliner). No AI gibberish text or fake license plates.
```
