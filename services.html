THE 8 CORE ROAD FREIGHT SERVICE CATEGORIES

Carefully engineered dispatch options covering standard dry van freight, consolidated shipments, long-haul corridors, and specialized vehicle operations.

SERVICE 01 // DEDICATED CAPACITY

1. Full Truckload (FTL)

For cargo requiring the entire capacity of a dedicated commercial truck. Direct point-to-point transit eliminates intermediate dock handling, reducing risk of cargo damage and cutting total transit time.

Suitable Cargo Examples:

Full factory production runs, high-volume palletized retail inventory, bulk packaging materials, and single-consignee shipments.

Important Enquiry Requirements:

Gross cargo weight, pallet dimensions, dock loading access at pickup/destination, and preferred departure window.

Request an FTL Quote
SERVICE 02 // CONSOLIDATED FREIGHT

2. Less Than Truckload (LTL)

For shipments that use part of a truck's available capacity. Cargo is securely palletized and combined with compatible freight along shared transit corridors to optimize cost efficiency.

Suitable Cargo Examples:

1 to 6 standard pallets, crated components, spare parts batches, and small distributor replenishments.

Important Enquiry Requirements:

Exact pallet counts, individual pallet height/weight, stackability status, and liftgate requirements.

Request an LTL Quote
SERVICE 03 // CONTRACT CARRIAGE

3. Dedicated Trucking

Transportation arrangements designed around recurring or dedicated freight requirements. Vehicles and drivers are scheduled on committed lanes to provide private-fleet performance without capital investment.

Suitable Cargo Examples:

Daily factory-to-warehouse transfers, scheduled vendor milk-runs, and regular multi-stop regional distribution.

Important Enquiry Requirements:

Dispatch frequency (daily/weekly), round-trip vs. one-way requirements, and specific equipment specs.

Discuss Dedicated Carriage
SERVICE 04 // INTERSTATE CORRIDORS

4. Long-Distance Trucking

Long-distance road freight enquiries across major industrial highways. Mapped waypoints, regulated rest intervals, and relay driver capability ensure predictable arrival windows across long stretches.

Suitable Cargo Examples:

Cross-country industrial equipment, bulk consumer items, raw material shipments, and capital goods.

Important Enquiry Requirements:

Total transit mileage, required delivery cutoff dates, transit insurance parameters, and receiving dock hours.

Enquire Long-Distance Transit
SERVICE 05 // OPEN DECK & OVERSIZED

5. Flatbed Transportation

For suitable oversized or irregularly shaped cargo, subject to verified vehicle and legal requirements. Offers top and side crane loading capabilities with heavy-duty ratchet and chain securement.

Suitable Cargo Examples:

Structural steel beams, pipe bundles, heavy industrial fabrications, precast panels, and lumber bundles.

Important Enquiry Requirements:

Exact dimensions (L x W x H), tie-down hook points, weather tarping needs, and crane loading schedule.

Request a Flatbed Quote
SERVICE 06 // CLIMATE CONTROLLED

6. Refrigerated Transportation

Temperature-controlled freight enquiries, subject to verified equipment and operating capability. Advanced thermostatic units maintain constant chilled or frozen conditions throughout the highway transit.

Suitable Cargo Examples:

Perishable foodstuffs, dairy, pharmaceutical serums, confectionary, and climate-sensitive chemical compounds.

Important Enquiry Requirements:

Required setpoint temperature (°C), allowable temperature variance tolerance, and pre-cooling confirmation.

Request a Reefer Quote
SERVICE 07 // INTERMODAL DRAYAGE

7. Container Transportation

Road movement enquiries for containerized freight. Chassis haulage connecting maritime container terminals, inland container depots (ICDs), and industrial manufacturing facilities.

Suitable Cargo Examples:

20ft standard, 40ft standard, and 40ft high-cube ISO shipping containers containing import/export goods.

Important Enquiry Requirements:

Container size, tare weight, gross cargo weight, port gate-in / cut-off deadlines, and customs clearance staging.

Request Container Drayage
SERVICE 08 // SPECIALIZED HEAVY HAULAGE

8. Industrial & Heavy Cargo

Specialized movement enquiries for industrial and heavy cargo, subject to equipment, permits, route, and load feasibility. Low-bed, multi-axle trailers configured to handle heavy concentrated payloads.

Suitable Cargo Examples:

Electrical transformers, heavy earthmoving equipment, industrial turbines, reactors, and oversized storage vessels.

Important Enquiry Requirements:

Detailed technical CAD/dimension drawing, center of gravity location, total tonnage, and route clearance feasibility.

Enquire Heavy Haulage
