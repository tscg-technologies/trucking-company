OPERATIONAL TIMELINE

THE FREIGHT MOVEMENT PROCESS

A structured four-milestone sequence from initial load qualification to final roadside delivery coordination.

01

Share Cargo Requirements

Submit gross weight, dimensions, pallet quantities, departure origin, and delivery destination parameters.

02

Review Transportation Needs

Our operations team evaluates equipment feasibility, axle load limits, loading dock capabilities, and timeline demands.

03

Plan the Movement

Corridor routes are surveyed, staging checkpoints verified, and equipment scheduled for prompt pickup.

04

Coordinate Delivery

En route vehicle progress is managed with constant status updates until safe unloading at the receiving facility.
